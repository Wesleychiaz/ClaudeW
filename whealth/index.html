001❤️

Heart Rate Before Sleep

Sleep · Free · Daily

▼
What it is

Your heart rate (bpm) measured lying quietly right before sleep. A single number reflecting your fitness, metabolic health, stress, and food habits.

Target Scores
~50 bpmExcellent
60–79 bpmImprove
80+ bpmIntervene
What Raises It
  • Alcohol before bed: +5–10 bpm
  • Stress & anxiety: +5–25 bpm
  • Large evening meals: +10 bpm
  • Late caffeine (after 12pm): +2–5 bpm
  • Intense evening exercise: +4–10 bpm
  • Dehydration or heat: +5 bpm
Protocol
  • Stop eating 4 hours before bed
  • Cut caffeine by 12pm
  • No intense exercise within 4 hours of bed
  • Screens off 1 hour before bed
  • Cool, dark, quiet room + same bedtime nightly
002⏱️

Intermittent Fasting

Diet · Free · Daily

▼
What it is

Cycling between defined eating and fasting windows. Not about what you eat — about when. Eating earlier directly lowers your pre-sleep heart rate.

Protocols
16:8Best to start
18:6Intermediate
5:2Flexible days
Recommended Start (16:8)
  • Eating window: 12pm – 8pm
  • Fast overnight + morning (16 hours)
  • Black coffee & water allowed during fast
  • Don't break fast with a large meal
