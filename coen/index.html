👋 Helping Coen at home

This is a playful warm-up for the speech sounds and describing skills Coen is working on. A few tips:

  • Model, don't drill. Say the word correctly and let him copy — keep it light and fun.
  • Use the record button. Hearing himself next to the model helps him notice the target sound.
  • Praise the try, not just the perfect answer. 3–5 minutes a day beats long sessions.
  • Stuck on /s/? "Don't drop your snake tail!" — gentle reminders work better than correction.
