About
Wesley Chia

I'm a Programme Manager at GovTech Singapore who builds in the margins — turning everyday problems into small tools, one weekend at a time.

Most of these projects started as something in my own life: a health scare, a mortgage decision, helping people I love learn something new. So I built a tool for it — with Claude as my pair, shipping fast and keeping it personal.

I care about making AI feel useful and human, not hype-y. If it doesn't make a real day a little better, it doesn't ship.
