👋 Exploring dinosaurs together

This is a playful first encyclopaedia of dinosaurs — built to feed a young dino-fan's curiosity. A few ideas:

  • Say the names out loud. Tap “Say my name” and copy it together — big science words are fun to master!
  • Look at the shapes. Each dino is drawn so kids learn to recognise plates, frills, horns, sails and clubs.
  • Plant-eater or meat-eater? The colour-coded badges teach herbivore / carnivore at a glance.
  • Play the Dino Quiz once you’ve met a few — it rewards looking closely.

Names are read by your device’s built-in voice. Everything runs on the device — nothing is uploaded, and it works offline once loaded.
