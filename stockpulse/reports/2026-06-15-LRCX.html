Company Overview

Lam Research designs and manufactures etch and deposition equipment — two of the most repeated steps in chip fabrication — making it an indispensable Enabler in the AI semiconductor supply chain. Every advanced chip (TSMC N3/N2, HBM, NAND) passes through Lam's chambers dozens of times per wafer, and as 3D architectures and atomic-layer processing proliferate, the number of Lam-performed steps per chip grows alongside complexity.

Bull Case

  • Exceptional revenue momentum: $21.68B TTM revenue grew 23.8% YoY — nearly twice the pace of the broader equipment market — with earnings growth of 40.8% YoY demonstrating strong operating leverage as the cycle accelerates. FCF of $4.35B against $4.75B cash and just $3.73B debt leaves the balance sheet in excellent shape.
  • HBM is a structural tailwind, not a trade: Every HBM stack requires dozens of Lam etch and deposition steps per die. With AI accelerator demand driving explosive HBM capacity expansion (SK Hynix, Samsung, Micron all ramping), Lam's SAM per wafer start is rising — not just the number of wafer starts.
  • Still within reach of analyst targets: At $366.81 with a consensus mean of $323.38 and high target of $425, there is ~16% upside to the bull case from 32 analysts. Compare this to KLAC, which has already run past all analyst targets — Lam has more room on the table.
  • Solid margins with upside as product mix shifts: Gross margin of 50.0% and operating margin of 35.0% are strong for capital equipment; as higher-value atomic-layer and GAA-era tools mix up, these should trend further higher.

Bear Case

  • China concentration risk is the biggest single variable: Historically ~30% of Lam's revenue came from China; ongoing BIS export restrictions specifically targeting advanced etch and deposition tools could directly impair a meaningful revenue segment with limited near-term alternative markets.
  • Stock up 312% over 1 year from a depressed base: LRCX was at $87.75 at its 52-week low — the recovery is extraordinary, but at $366.81 it now sits at 98% of its 52-week high. At 69.5x TTM P/E and 46.0x forward P/E, any guidance softness could trigger a sharp derating.
  • Memory oversupply cycles cut fast: Lam's NAND exposure is significant; if memory manufacturers slow capex in response to pricing pressure (as they have historically), etch and deposition orders drop quickly and WFE estimates can be revised down 20-30% in a quarter.

Verdict

BUY — Cycle Momentum With Room to Run

Lam is the picks-and-shovels beneficiary of both the AI chip buildout and the HBM memory supercycle, with 23.8% revenue growth, 40.8% earnings growth, and a cleaner valuation setup than peer KLAC (still within reach of analyst targets). China export risk is real and worth monitoring, but the structural demand case — more Lam steps per chip as nodes shrink — overrides near-term regulatory noise. Time horizon: 12-18 months; sized appropriately given beta of 1.87.
