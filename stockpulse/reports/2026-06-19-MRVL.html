AI Analysis

What Marvell does: Marvell Technology is a pure-play AI Enabler — it designs the silicon that powers AI infrastructure rather than running AI workloads itself. Its two core businesses are custom AI ASICs (co-designed with hyperscalers like Amazon and Google to accelerate training and inference) and data center networking chips (optical DSPs, PAM4 transceiver ICs, and Ethernet switching silicon). As hyperscalers move toward custom silicon to escape Nvidia pricing power, Marvell is one of a very small number of foundries-agnostic chip architects that can win those engagements.
