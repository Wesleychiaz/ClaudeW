Company Overview

KLA Corporation is the world's dominant semiconductor process control and yield management company, supplying inspection and metrology equipment that detects nanometer-scale defects during chip fabrication. As a category-defining Enabler, KLA is deeply embedded in every major foundry's production line — TSMC, Samsung, Intel Foundry Services — and its criticality compounds as nodes shrink to 2nm and below, where defect economics make yield control more valuable per wafer than ever.
