Company Overview

Micron Technology is one of only three companies globally that manufacture DRAM and NAND flash memory at scale, alongside Samsung and SK Hynix. As an AI Enabler, Micron sits at the infrastructure layer of the AI stack — its High Bandwidth Memory (HBM3E) chips are critical components inside NVIDIA H100/H200/Blackwell GPUs and AMD MI300 accelerators, making it a direct picks-and-shovels play on AI compute demand. The company operates across Cloud, Data Center, Mobile/Client, and Automotive/Embedded segments.
