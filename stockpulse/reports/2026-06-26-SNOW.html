Company Overview

Snowflake is the dominant cloud-native data platform, enabling enterprises to consolidate, share, and query massive datasets across AWS, Azure, and GCP without managing infrastructure. As an AI Enabler, it sits upstream of the AI stack — before models can be trained or queried against enterprise data, that data must live somewhere governed and accessible, which is exactly what Snowflake's AI Data Cloud provides. CEO Sridhar Ramaswamy (former Google SVP of Ads) has aggressively repositioned the platform around AI workloads, with Cortex AI allowing customers to run LLMs and vector search directly on their Snowflake data.
